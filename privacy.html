Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arqen ("Arqen," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Arqen mobile app (the "App") and related services.

Information we collect

Account information. Name, email address, phone number, and profile details you provide when you create an account, including via Sign in with Apple.

Project and design data. Room scans, floor plans, 3D designs, saved projects, photos you upload, and consultation or financing requests.

Location. Approximate or precise location, used to show local weather, nearby land listings, and to preview designs on your actual lot. You control location access in your device settings.

Camera and photo library. Used for LiDAR room scanning, AR preview, and uploading photos for your projects. Camera and photo access only occurs when you actively use these features.

Messages. Content you send through in-app chat with your project manager, contractor, design lead, or Arqen Support.

Device and usage information. Device type, operating system, app version, push notification token, and general usage data to keep the App working and secure.

Payment information. Subscription purchases (Arqen Vision Pass) are processed by Apple and our subscription provider; we do not store your full payment card details.

How we use your information

  • Provide core App features: room scanning, design tools, AR preview, land listings, and messaging.
  • Connect you with your project team and manage your renovation, build, or design project.
  • Send push notifications about messages, milestones, and project updates.
  • Process Vision Pass subscriptions and manage your account.
  • Respond to support requests and improve the App.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect the security of our services.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Members of your assigned Arqen project team (project manager, contractor, design lead) for the purpose of delivering your project.
  • Service providers who help us operate the App, including cloud hosting and database infrastructure (Supabase), push notification delivery (Apple APNs), and subscription management (RevenueCat).
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Arqen or others.

Data retention

We retain your account and project data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services, complete a project, or meet legal and accounting obligations. You can request deletion at any time.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your profile information from Profile → Account Settings within the App.
  • You can request full account deletion in-app or by emailing support@arqenhause.com.
  • You can disable camera, location, photo library, or notification permissions at any time in your device settings; some features will not work without them.
  • You can manage or cancel Arqen Vision Pass through your device's subscription settings.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls on our backend. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and material changes will be communicated through the App where appropriate.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@arqenhause.com.
