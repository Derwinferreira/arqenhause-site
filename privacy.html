Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Arqen Haus Corp. ("Arqen," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Arqen mobile application (the "App") and provides construction, renovation, and related design services (the "Services") in Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have, whether you interact with us through the App, our website at arqenhause.com, by phone, or in person as a construction client.

By using the App, our website, or engaging Arqen for construction or design services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App or our Services.

Information we collect

Account information. Name, email address, phone number, mailing/billing address, and profile details you provide when you create an account, including via Sign in with Apple.

Project and design data. Room and lot scans (including LiDAR scan data), floor plans, 3D designs, saved projects, photos and videos you upload, blueprints, material and finish selections, and any files you share with your project team.

Construction and contracting records. If you engage Arqen for a renovation, custom build, pool, landscaping, or other construction project, we collect information necessary to quote, contract, permit, schedule, and build your project, including property address and details, site surveys, permit applications and municipal filings, contractor and subcontractor assignments, change orders, project timelines and milestones, invoices, payment and deposit records, and warranty or service history.

Location. Approximate or precise location, used to show local weather, nearby land listings, to preview designs on your actual lot in AR, and to coordinate site visits and deliveries for active construction projects. You control location access in your device settings.

Camera and photo library. Used for LiDAR room and lot scanning, AR preview, and uploading photos or videos for your projects, site documentation, and progress updates. Camera and photo access only occurs when you actively use these features.

Messages and communications. Content you send through in-app chat with your project manager, contractor, or design lead, and any communications with Arqen Support by phone, email, or in person.

Financing and consultation requests. Information you submit when booking a consultation or requesting financing options, which we may share with third-party lenders or partners at your request.

Device and usage information. Device type, operating system, app version, unique device identifiers, push notification token, IP address, and general usage/analytics data to keep the App working, secure, and improving.

Payment information. Vision Pass subscription purchases are processed by Apple and our subscription provider (RevenueCat); we do not store your full payment card details. Construction project payments, deposits, and invoices may be processed through third-party payment processors or directly with Arqen, and we retain records of amounts, dates, and payment status as required for accounting and legal purposes.

How we use your information

  • Provide core App features: room and lot scanning, design tools, AR preview, land listings, and messaging.
  • Quote, contract, schedule, permit, staff, and deliver renovation, custom home, pool, and landscaping projects.
  • Connect you with your assigned project manager, contractor, and design lead, and coordinate site visits.
  • Send push notifications, emails, or texts about messages, quotes, milestones, permits, and project updates.
  • Process Vision Pass subscriptions and manage your account.
  • Process invoices, deposits, and payments related to construction services.
  • Respond to support requests, warranty claims, and improve the App and our Services.
  • Comply with building codes, permitting requirements, contracts, and other legal obligations, and protect the security of our services, staff, and clients.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Members of your assigned Arqen project team (project manager, contractor, design lead, trades, and subcontractors) as needed to deliver your project.
  • Municipal authorities, building departments, and permit offices, where required to obtain permits or approvals for your construction project.
  • Service providers who help us operate the App and our business, including cloud hosting and database infrastructure (Supabase), push notification delivery (Apple APNs), subscription management (RevenueCat), mapping services (Mapbox), and accounting, payment, or financing partners.
  • Authorities, when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Arqen, our clients, or others.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to the same privacy commitments described here.

Data retention

We retain account and app usage data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. For construction projects, we retain contracts, permits, invoices, warranty records, and related project documentation for the period required by applicable law, professional standards, warranty terms, and our insurance requirements, which may extend well beyond project completion. You can request deletion of account and app data at any time, subject to records we are legally required to keep.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your profile information from Profile → Account Settings within the App.
  • You can request account and app data deletion in-app or by emailing info@arqenhause.com. Note that construction contract, permit, financial, and warranty records may need to be retained separately as required by law.
  • You can disable camera, location, photo library, or notification permissions at any time in your device settings; some features will not work without them.
  • You can manage or cancel Arqen Vision Pass through your device's subscription settings.
  • You can opt out of marketing emails or texts at any time; you will still receive essential communications about an active construction project or contract.

Children's privacy

The App and our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, access controls on our backend, and restricted access to project and financial records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@arqenhause.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and material changes will be communicated through the App or our website where appropriate.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to info@arqenhause.com.
